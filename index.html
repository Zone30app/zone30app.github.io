How it works

  • 📍
    Real-time GPS tracking The app uses your phone's GPS to track your position as you drive, with no data sent to any server.
  • 🏫
    School zone detection Automatically loads schools and educational institutions near you using OpenStreetMap — a free, community-maintained database.
  • 🔔
    Multi-mode alerts Choose sound, vibration, and/or a visual pop-up banner. Get an early warning at 450 m and a full alert at 250 m from the zone.
  • 🗓️
    Smart schedule Alerts only activate when restrictions actually apply: Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM, September 1 through June 30.
  • 🔒
    100% private No account required. No data collected. Your location never leaves your device. Zone 30 works entirely on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Does Zone 30 work without internet?
The GPS tracking works offline. An internet connection is needed once when you start monitoring to download the list of schools near you. After that, it works without data.
Why does it need my location?
Location access is the core function of the app — it's how Zone 30 knows when you're approaching a school zone. Your location is never stored or shared.
What if a school isn't in the database?
Zone 30 uses OpenStreetMap data. If a school is missing, anyone can add it at openstreetmap.org — it's a community-maintained map used by millions worldwide.
Does it work while I use Google Maps?
Keep Zone 30 open in a separate tab or split screen alongside your navigation app. The alerts will still sound even if your screen is on another app, as long as the browser tab stays active.
Is it free?
Yes, Zone 30 is completely free to use. It's supported by ads, which help keep the service running at no cost to you.
